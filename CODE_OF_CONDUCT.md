# Code of conduct

This policy applies to Agenda's project-managed issues, pull requests and
community spaces, including interactions among users, maintainers and contributors.

Treat people respectfully, welcome different backgrounds and experience levels,
and critique code and ideas constructively. Respect privacy and personal
boundaries. Never publish personal calendar data or contact information without
permission. Accept corrections and keep discussions relevant.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, sexual
harassment and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

For visible misconduct, use platform reporting tools where available. A private
maintainer conduct-reporting address is not configured. Request a private contact
without identifying affected people or posting sensitive details publicly.
Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, warn participants, close or lock
disruptive conversations or restrict participation according to severity and
repetition. Reports should be handled with discretion and fairness. Platform
operators may apply their own policies. Public reports have no confidentiality
or response-time guarantee.
